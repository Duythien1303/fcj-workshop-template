### Introduction

During my internship at **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)**, I had the opportunity to explore and study technologies related to Cloud Computing, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

The internship program is designed to help students strengthen their technical knowledge, develop independent learning and research skills, and become familiar with technologies and workflows used in professional working environments.

Internship activities include studying technical documentation, exploring AWS services, participating in learning activities, and developing essential skills for working in the information technology industry.

This worklog is organized by week to document research activities, assigned tasks, and achievements throughout the internship.

### Weekly Worklog Overview

#### Phase 1: AWS Technology Research and Exploration

**Week 1:** [Introduction to Cloud Computing and Amazon Web Services (AWS)](1.1-week1/)

- Become familiar with the First Cloud AI Journey internship program.
- Study Cloud Computing, IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS.
- Explore AWS Global Infrastructure and the AWS Shared Responsibility Model.
- Become familiar with the AWS Management Console.

**Week 2:** [AWS Networking & Security](1.2-week2/)

- Explore Amazon VPC, Subnets, and Route Tables.
- Study Internet Gateways, Security Groups, and Network ACLs.
- Learn about AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM).
- Research security principles and access management.

**Week 3:** [AWS Storage & Database](1.3-week3/)

- Study Amazon S3, Amazon EBS, and Amazon EFS.
- Differentiate between Object Storage, Block Storage, and File Storage.
- Explore Amazon RDS and Amazon DynamoDB.
- Study data security, backup, and management methods.

**Week 4:** [AWS Serverless & Application Deployment](1.4-week4/)

- Understand Serverless Computing architecture.
- Explore AWS Lambda and Amazon API Gateway.
- Study Amazon CloudWatch and application monitoring methods.
- Research the process of designing and deploying Serverless applications.

**Week 5:** [Generative AI on AWS](1.5-week5/)

- Explore Generative AI, Large Language Models, and Foundation Models.
- Study Amazon Bedrock and Prompt Engineering.
- Understand Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Knowledge Bases.
- Explore AI Agents and Agentic AI.
- Study methods for evaluating and deploying Generative AI applications.
