### 1. Overview of the Internship

During my internship at **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)**, I had the opportunity to explore and study knowledge and technologies related to cloud computing and artificial intelligence on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) platform.

Through learning activities, researching documentation, and participating in the internship program, I gradually developed a foundational understanding of Cloud Computing. I also learned about common AWS services such as Amazon VPC, Amazon S3, Amazon RDS, AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, and Amazon CloudWatch.

In addition, I had the opportunity to explore modern artificial intelligence technologies, including Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Amazon Bedrock, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI Agents.

The internship helped me improve my self-learning skills, ability to research technical documentation, problem analysis, and work organization. At the same time, I became more aware of the skills I need to improve to meet the expectations of a professional working environment.

### 3. Personal Strengths

#### 3.1. Self-learning and Research Skills

During the internship, I recognized self-learning and research as some of my personal strengths.

I can proactively look for resources in AWS Documentation, AWS Skill Builder, and other relevant learning materials to explore new technologies.

Researching different groups of AWS services helped me gradually develop a systematic learning approach, from understanding basic concepts to studying architectures and real-world use cases.

#### 3.2. Initiative and Responsibility

I consistently try to take initiative in completing assigned tasks, developing learning plans, and monitoring my research progress.

I also place importance on taking notes and summarizing knowledge to support review and the preparation of my internship report.

#### 3.3. Adaptability to New Technologies

Through the internship program, I had the opportunity to explore several technologies that I had not previously studied in depth, especially Cloud Computing services and Generative AI on AWS.

This process helped me strengthen my ability to learn new concepts, become familiar with technical terminology, and adapt to rapidly evolving technologies.

### 4. Areas for Improvement

#### 4.1. Practical Experience and System Deployment

Although I have studied the fundamentals of AWS services, I recognize that I still need to gain more hands-on experience.

Researching documentation has helped me establish a theoretical foundation; however, I still need more practice in designing, deploying, and operating a complete system.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Gain more hands-on experience through labs and workshops.
- Proactively experiment with simple AWS architectures in authorized environments.
- Explore how multiple AWS services can be integrated into a single system.
- Practice testing, monitoring, and troubleshooting deployed applications.

#### 4.2. Communication and Presentation Skills

I recognize that I need to continue improving my ability to explain technical content clearly, concisely, and understandably.

In particular, presenting system architectures and explaining how new technologies work are skills that require regular practice.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Proactively communicate and ask questions when encountering unclear issues.
- Practice presenting the knowledge I have researched.
- Improve my technical documentation and report-writing skills.
- Participate more actively in discussions and knowledge-sharing activities.

#### 4.3. Analytical Thinking and Problem-solving Skills

While exploring new technologies, I have noticed that I sometimes rely too heavily on existing tutorials and guides.

I need to strengthen my ability to analyze the causes of problems, evaluate different approaches, and independently choose suitable solutions.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Practice breaking down problems into smaller, manageable parts.
- Investigate the root causes of errors instead of only applying existing solutions.
- Compare the advantages and limitations of technologies before making decisions.
- Gain more practice through real-world scenarios.

#### 4.4. Time Management Skills

I need to continue improving how I allocate time among researching new concepts, hands-on practice, and completing assigned tasks.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Create specific study and work plans.
- Prioritize tasks based on their importance.
- Monitor work progress on a weekly basis.
- Set aside time to summarize and evaluate results after each stage.

### 5. Personal Development Goals

Moving forward, I aim to further develop my knowledge and skills in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

My development goals include:

- **Strengthening AWS Knowledge:** Continue exploring AWS services and best practices for designing Cloud architectures.
- **Improving Programming Skills:** Practice Python and technologies used in application development.
- **Gaining More Practical Experience:** Apply what I have learned through labs and real-world projects.
- **Expanding AI Knowledge:** Continue studying Machine Learning, Generative AI, and methods for integrating AI into applications.
- **Developing Soft Skills:** Improve communication, teamwork, time management, and problem-solving skills.
- **Strengthening Research Skills:** Improve my ability to locate, analyze, and synthesize technical documentation.

### 6. Conclusion

Through my internship at First Cloud AI Journey, I had the opportunity to explore and expand my knowledge of Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

Studying Amazon Web Services helped me better understand the fundamental components of a cloud computing system, including Networking, Security, Storage, Database, Serverless Computing, and Generative AI.

In addition to technical knowledge, the internship program helped me develop self-learning skills, initiative, and a sense of responsibility at work.

However, I also recognize the need to continue improving my practical experience, communication skills, problem-solving abilities, and time management.

I consider the knowledge and experience gained during this internship an important foundation for my continued personal development and preparation for a professional career in information technology.
